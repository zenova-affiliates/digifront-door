Transparent Workflow

From initial conversation to live website in 9 simple steps.

We take care of all the technical heavy lifting—design, coding, speed optimization, and Google profile linking—while keeping you in full control.

1

Initial Inquiry & Profile Audit

You reach out via our contact form or phone with basic details about your business. We review your existing Google Business Profile, check how competitors in your area are positioned, and note immediate improvement areas.

✓ Free profile assessment & scope summary
2

Requirement Discovery & Strategy

We confirm what services generate your highest margins, who your ideal customers are, and whether you need online booking, click-to-call prioritization, or a gallery showcase.

✓ Clear sitemap & page structure
3

Transparent Proposal & Milestone Plan

You receive a fixed-price proposal specifying deliverables, revision rounds, and an exact timeline. No hidden charges or recurring software platform locks.

✓ Agreed scope, fixed price, and deposit confirmation
4

Design Mockup & Approval

We create a high-fidelity visual layout tailored to your business aesthetic. You review the desktop and mobile views, and we make adjustments until you are completely satisfied with the look and feel.

✓ Approved visual design before writing code
5

Agile Development & Coding

We build the website using clean, modern semantic HTML5, fluid responsive CSS, and minimal vanilla JavaScript. Everything is built to render instantly on mobile networks.

✓ Zero-bloat, W3C-valid, responsive code
6

Content Integration & Local SEO Architecture

We populate your text, photos, reviews, and service descriptions. We embed Schema.org LocalBusiness structured data, Open Graph tags, and set up your XML sitemap for search engines.

✓ Search-engine-ready content & meta tags
7

Client Review & Quality Revisions

You receive a private staging link to test the site on your phone and computer. We test all phone call links, contact forms, and verify that information is 100% accurate.

✓ Staging link review & revisions completed
8

Domain Setup, SSL & Live Launch

We configure your custom domain name (e.g., yourbusiness.com), issue free SSL security certificates, and make your website live to the world.

✓ Secure HTTPS live deployment
9

Google Profile Sync & Ongoing Care

We connect the live website URL and appointment booking links directly to your Google Business Profile. We submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and provide optional maintenance care.

✓ Complete project handover & ongoing support options
Clear Roles

What We Need From You vs. What We Handle

We keep your time commitment minimal so you can focus on running your business.

What We Handle For You:

  • Complete website UI/UX layout and visual styling
  • Full responsive frontend development and mobile testing
  • Image compression and WebP conversion for fast loading
  • Contact form setup, spam protection, and email delivery
  • Local SEO schema markup and Google sitemap generation
  • Domain DNS connection and SSL certificate installation
  • Step-by-step guidance on linking to your Google profile

What We Need From You:

  • A 15-minute conversation regarding your business goals
  • Your logo, company phone number, and address
  • Any existing photos of your team, work, or clinic (we can supply curated imagery if needed)
  • Feedback on design mockups and staging previews
