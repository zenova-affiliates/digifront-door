Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about our website packages, Google integration, pricing, and timelines.

Your Google profile gets you noticed in local search results, but up to 60% of searchers click "Website" before deciding to call. If you don't have a website—or if the link is broken or slow—they assume you are either unprofessional or closed, and they call the next business on the list. A website validates your reputation, answers questions upfront, and closes the deal.

Our packages are straightforward: our Starter Website is $299 (one-time), our multi-page Business Website is $599 (one-time), and Growth / Custom platforms start at $999. There are no recurring monthly software subscriptions required to keep your site live.

Yes, 100%. Once final payment is made, you own all the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, graphics, and content. You are free to move it to any web host or developer at any time without restriction.

A Starter Website typically takes 5 to 7 business days from the moment we receive your information. Standard Business Websites take 10 to 14 business days. Custom and e-commerce platforms take 2 to 3 weeks depending on the complexity of features requested.

No honest web developer can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google, as rankings depend on competition, physical proximity to the searcher, and your business's overall review history. What we do guarantee is building a technically superior foundation: sub-second loading speeds, semantic schema markup, clean mobile UX, and full alignment with Google's search algorithms.

You purchase your domain directly from a registrar like Cloudflare, Namecheap, or Google (typically $10–$15 per year) so that you remain the legal owner. For hosting, we can deploy your website on high-speed static cloud infrastructure (which often costs $0 to $5/month) or onto your existing web host. We configure all DNS and SSL settings for you.

All projects come with included post-launch warranty support (14 to 30 days depending on package). For ongoing needs, you can subscribe to one of our Care & Maintenance plans (from $39/mo) which includes dedicated update hours, or request hourly updates whenever you need them.

We work on a milestone basis: 50% deposit upon approving the proposal to begin design and development, and the remaining 50% upon final client sign-off and before live domain deployment. We accept direct bank transfer, Wise, and card payments.
