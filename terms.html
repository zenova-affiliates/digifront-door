Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026. Clear, fair terms governing our web development and integration services.

1. Scope & Project Estimates

All project quotes and website calculations provided on this website are baseline estimates. Binding work commences once a formal proposal specifying exact deliverables, revision counts, and milestones is signed or agreed upon in writing by both parties.

2. Payment Milestones

Standard projects require a 50% non-refundable commencement deposit before initial design concepts and development begin. The remaining 50% balance is payable upon final staging approval and prior to live domain deployment or release of final source files.

3. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Upon receipt of full and final payment, complete intellectual property rights to the custom HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and graphics developed specifically for the client's project are transferred to the client. The client retains full ownership of their text, branding, and imagery.

4. Revisions & Scope Changes

Each package includes designated rounds of revisions (e.g. 2 rounds for Starter, 3 for Business) during the design and staging phase. Revisions that request fundamentally new functionality, additional pages beyond the agreed package, or substantial redesigns after approval are quoted as separate scope add-ons.

5. Search Rankings & Google Policies

DigiFront Door builds technically compliant, high-speed websites adhering to Google's best practices. However, we do not guarantee specific numerical search rankings, local pack positions, or revenue amounts, as Google's algorithms operate independently and consider factors outside any developer's direct control.

6. Inquiries & Contact

For questions regarding our terms or project agreements, please email us directly at digifrontdoor7@gmail.com.
