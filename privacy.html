Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. How DigiFront Door respects and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form or quote calculator, we collect the details you voluntarily provide: your name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, business name, and project description. We do not collect sensitive payment data directly on this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to evaluate your project scope, prepare quotations, schedule consultations, and deliver website services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact details with any third-party marketing companies.

3. Data Retention & Security

We maintain administrative and technical measures to protect your inquiries. Inquiries sent through our form are transmitted securely over HTTPS directly to our verified email address (digifrontdoor7@gmail.com).

4. Third-Party Services & Fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads typography from Google Fonts. These services may collect technical connection metrics (such as your IP address and browser user agent) for server delivery and security logging.

5. Your Rights

You may at any time request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or request that your contact history be deleted from our records. To exercise your rights, email us at digifrontdoor7@gmail.com.
