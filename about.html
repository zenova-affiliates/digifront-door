Our Story & Philosophy

We help local businesses turn Google searches into phone calls.

DigiFront Door was created to solve a widespread problem: great businesses losing high-value local clients simply because their Google profile has no proper website.

The Missing Door in Local Business

Consider how people find services today. When someone needs a dentist, an emergency plumber, an architectural joiner, or a trusted local accountant, they open Google Maps. They look for 5-star reviews, customer photos, and operating hours.

Then, they tap the Website button.

In far too many cases, that button either doesn't exist, links to an unformatted social media page, or leads to a heavy website that takes 6 seconds to open on a phone. The customer gets frustrated, hits "Back", and calls your competitor instead.

"Our mission is simple: we build clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites that seamlessly connect to your Google profile, so potential customers stop leaving and start calling."

What Sets DigiFront Door Apart

Zero Bloat Architecture

We don't use heavy website builders that inject 5MB of scripts for a simple 3-page site. We handcraft clean, semantic HTML, modern CSS, and minimal JavaScript that loads in under a second.

100% Code Ownership

You are never trapped in a proprietary monthly website builder subscription. When the project is completed, you own all the files, code, and assets with complete freedom.

Thumb-Friendly Mobile UX

Most local searches happen while someone is walking down the street or in their car. We position prominent phone dialer links, WhatsApp buttons, and directions right where thumbs reach naturally.

Direct Developer Partnership

No account managers playing broken telephone. You speak directly with the developer designing and writing your site code.

Our Quality Principles

  • Honest Promises: We will never guarantee overnight #1 rankings on Google or make false claims about instant wealth. We build technically sound, fast foundations that earn customer trust.
  • Accessibility for Everyone: We follow WCAG accessibility guidelines—clear contrast, keyboard-navigable menus, and proper screen-reader labels.
  • Transparent Pricing: Upfront package pricing without surprise fees or locked contracts.
